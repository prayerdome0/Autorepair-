About SWDL

SWDL is the automotive arm of Seedwel Investment Limited — built to earn one thing above all else: your trust behind the wheel.

Our story

Built by Seedwel, Run by Mechanics

SWDL Auto Repair was established by Seedwel Investment Limited with one clear brief: run the kind of workshop the founders would happily leave their own family cars with.

What started as a two-bay garage has grown into a full-service workshop serving private drivers, taxi and delivery fleets, and commercial accounts. The name SWDL — taken from Seedwel — stands for the standard we hold ourselves to on every single job.

Today, more than 12,000 vehicles later, the promise is unchanged: diagnose properly, quote honestly, and only fit genuine parts.

Milestones

The SWDL Journey

2019

SWDL is founded

Seedwel Investment Limited opens a two-bay workshop with one lift, one compressor and a promise: no car leaves until it's right.

2021

The workshop expands

A second lift, a laser alignment rig and a full OBD-II diagnostic suite come online as the customer base grows by word of mouth.

2023

10,000 vehicles serviced

Fleet accounts join the regulars and SWDL launches its 24/7 roadside assistance line.

2025

The detailing bay opens

Paint correction, machine polish and interior deep-cleaning round out the full-service offering.
